### I. Multiple Choice Questions
*Reference: Textbook Pages 5 to 11 — Spatial Comparisons*

1. Which position describes a bird sitting at the highest point of a tree?
   - (A) Top
   - (B) Bottom
   - (C) Under
   - (D) Outside

2. What word describes a dog staying inside its kennel?
   - (A) Inside
   - (B) Outside
   - (C) Below
   - (D) Far

3. A book is kept resting on a study table. Which word describes its position?
   - (A) On
   - (B) Under
   - (C) Outside
   - (D) Far

4. An aeroplane is flying in the sky high in the air. Which term describes its position?
   - (A) Above
   - (B) Below
   - (C) Under
   - (D) Inside

5. A student is standing very close to the school gate. Which term correctly describes the distance?
   - (A) Near
   - (B) Far
   - (C) Outside
   - (D) Under

6. An elephant is huge, while a mouse is tiny. Which comparison pair describes them?
   - (A) Big - Small
   - (B) Top - Bottom
   - (C) Inside - Outside
   - (D) Near - Far

### II. Fill in the Blanks
*Reference: Textbook Pages 5 to 14 — Geometric Comparisons and Basic Shapes*

1. The cat sitting under the chair is positioned _______ the chair. (on / under)
2. A ball placed outside the basket is located _______. (inside / outside)
3. A shape having 4 equal sides and 4 corners is called a _______. (square / rectangle)
4. A round shape without any sides or corners is a _______. (circle / triangle)
5. A triangle has exactly _______ corners. (three / four)
