### I. Multiple Choice Questions
*Reference: Textbook Pages 60 to 66 — Organizing Information*

1. What is the process of collecting and arranging items systematically called?
   - (A) Organizing Information
   - (B) Subtraction
   - (C) Estimation
   - (D) Measurement

2. In a classroom, there are 4 red balls, 3 blue balls, and 2 green balls. Which colour ball is highest in count?
   - (A) Red
   - (B) Blue
   - (C) Green
   - (D) Yellow

3. In a fruit basket with 5 apples, 6 bananas, and 2 mangoes, which fruit is least in number?
   - (A) Mango
   - (B) Banana
   - (C) Apple
   - (D) Orange
