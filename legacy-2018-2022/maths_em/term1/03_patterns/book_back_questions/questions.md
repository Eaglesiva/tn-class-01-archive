### I. Multiple Choice Questions
*Reference: Textbook Pages 54 to 59 — Patterns*

1. What comes next in the clapping rhythm: Clap, Snap, Clap, Snap, _______?
   - (A) Clap
   - (B) Snap
   - (C) Whistle
   - (D) Stomp

2. In a colour pattern: Red, Blue, Red, Blue, what colour comes next?
   - (A) Red
   - (B) Blue
   - (C) Green
   - (D) Yellow

3. In a shape sequence: Circle, Triangle, Circle, Triangle, which shape appears next?
   - (A) Circle
   - (B) Triangle
   - (C) Square
   - (D) Rectangle

4. What is a repeating arrangement of sounds, colours, or shapes called?
   - (A) Pattern
   - (B) Subtraction
   - (C) Measurement
   - (D) Digit
