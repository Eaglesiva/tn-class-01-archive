### I. Multiple Choice Questions
*Reference: Textbook Pages 15 to 36 — Numbers from 1 to 9 and Concept of Zero*

1. Which number represents the count of fingers on one hand?
   - (A) 5
   - (B) 4
   - (C) 6
   - (D) 7

2. Which number comes immediately before 8?
   - (A) 7
   - (B) 9
   - (C) 6
   - (D) 5

3. Which number comes immediately after 4?
   - (A) 5
   - (B) 3
   - (C) 6
   - (D) 2

4. Which number lies in between 6 and 8?
   - (A) 7
   - (B) 5
   - (C) 9
   - (D) 4

5. All three birds flew away from a branch. How many birds are left on the branch?
   - (A) 0
   - (B) 1
   - (C) 2
   - (D) 3

6. Which number is greater: 7 or 3?
   - (A) 7
   - (B) 3
   - (C) Both are equal
   - (D) Cannot be determined
