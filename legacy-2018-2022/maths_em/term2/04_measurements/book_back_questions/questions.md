### I. Multiple Choice Questions
*Reference: Textbook Pages 44 to 51 — Comparisons & Non-standard Measures*

1. When comparing two pencils, the one with greater length is described as:
   - (A) Longer
   - (B) Shorter
   - (C) Thicker
   - (D) Lighter

2. Comparing a coconut tree and a banana plant, which one is taller?
   - (A) Coconut tree
   - (B) Banana plant
   - (C) Both are equal
   - (D) Cannot be determined

3. When comparing a watermelon and an apple, which one is heavier?
   - (A) Watermelon
   - (B) Apple
   - (C) Both are equal
   - (D) Cannot be determined

4. What is the unit of length measured from the tip of the thumb to the tip of the little finger on an outstretched hand?
   - (A) Handspan
   - (B) Cubit
   - (C) Foot span
   - (D) Pace

5. What is the measurement from the elbow to the tip of the middle finger called?
   - (A) Cubit
   - (B) Handspan
   - (C) Foot span
   - (D) Pace

6. The distance covered in one normal walking step is called a:
   - (A) Pace
   - (B) Handspan
   - (C) Cubit
   - (D) Finger length
