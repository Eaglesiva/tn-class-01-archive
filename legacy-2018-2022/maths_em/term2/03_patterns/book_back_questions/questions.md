### I. Multiple Choice Questions
*Reference: Textbook Pages 38, 40, 42 — Patterns*

1. What is the next number in the sequence: 2, 4, 6, 8, _______?
   - (A) 10
   - (B) 9
   - (C) 12
   - (D) 7

2. What is the next number in the odd sequence: 1, 3, 5, 7, _______?
   - (A) 9
   - (B) 8
   - (C) 10
   - (D) 6

3. In the shape pattern: Square, Circle, Triangle, Square, Circle, which shape comes next?
   - (A) Triangle
   - (B) Rectangle
   - (C) Circle
   - (D) Square

4. In a movement pattern: Hand up, Hand down, Hand up, Hand down... what is the next movement?
   - (A) Hand up
   - (B) Jump
   - (C) Sit
   - (D) Turn
