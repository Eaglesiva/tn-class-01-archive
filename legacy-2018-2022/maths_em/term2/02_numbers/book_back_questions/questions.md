### I. Multiple Choice Questions
*Reference: Textbook Pages 16 to 36 — Numbers 10 to 20 & Addition*

1. A group of 10 ones is equal to:
   - (A) 1 Ten
   - (B) 2 Tens
   - (C) 10 Tens
   - (D) 0

2. Which number is formed by 1 Ten and 4 Ones?
   - (A) 14
   - (B) 41
   - (C) 15
   - (D) 24

3. What is the number that comes immediately before 15?
   - (A) 14
   - (B) 16
   - (C) 13
   - (D) 17

4. What is the number that comes immediately after 18?
   - (A) 19
   - (B) 17
   - (C) 20
   - (D) 16

5. Which number lies in between 12 and 14?
   - (A) 13
   - (B) 11
   - (C) 15
   - (D) 16

6. What is the sum of 8 + 7?
   - (A) 15
   - (B) 14
   - (C) 16
   - (D) 13

7. What is 10 + 6?
   - (A) 16
   - (B) 17
   - (C) 15
   - (D) 60

8. Which number is greater between 13 and 19?
   - (A) 19
   - (B) 13
   - (C) Both are equal
   - (D) Cannot be determined
