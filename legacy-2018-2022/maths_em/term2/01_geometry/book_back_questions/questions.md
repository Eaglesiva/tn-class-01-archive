### I. Multiple Choice Questions
*Reference: Textbook Pages 5 to 15 — Shapes and Lines*

1. Which shape has 4 equal sides and 4 corners?
   - (A) Square
   - (B) Rectangle
   - (C) Triangle
   - (D) Circle

2. Which shape has 3 sides and 3 corners?
   - (A) Triangle
   - (B) Circle
   - (C) Square
   - (D) Rectangle

3. Which curved closed shape has no corners and no straight edges?
   - (A) Circle
   - (B) Square
   - (C) Rectangle
   - (D) Triangle

4. Which shape has 4 sides where only opposite sides are equal?
   - (A) Rectangle
   - (B) Square
   - (C) Triangle
   - (D) Circle

5. Which types of lines form the English letter 'L'?
   - (A) Standing line and Sleeping line
   - (B) Slanting line only
   - (C) Curved line only
   - (D) Two curved lines

6. Which type of line is used to draw the English letter 'O'?
   - (A) Curved line
   - (B) Standing line
   - (C) Sleeping line
   - (D) Slanting line
