### I. Multiple Choice Questions
*Reference: Textbook Pages 48 to 54 — Daily Routine & Time*

1. What is the first healthy habit we do immediately after waking up in the morning?
   - (A) Brushing our teeth
   - (B) Going to school
   - (C) Going to sleep
   - (D) Eating dinner

2. What part of the day begins when the sun sets and darkness falls?
   - (A) Night
   - (B) Morning
   - (C) Noon
   - (D) Dawn

3. During which part of the 24 hours do we see the moon and twinkling stars in the sky?
   - (A) Night
   - (B) Noon
   - (C) Morning
   - (D) Evening

4. During which part of the day do school children usually play games outdoors?
   - (A) Evening
   - (B) Morning
   - (C) Midnight
   - (D) Noon

5. How many days are there in a complete week?
   - (A) 7
   - (B) 6
   - (C) 8
   - (D) 5
