### I. Multiple Choice Questions
*Reference: Textbook Pages 43, 44, 46 — Coins and Notes*

1. What is the value of the smallest standard coin in common daily use in India?
   - (A) ₹1
   - (B) ₹2
   - (C) ₹5
   - (D) ₹10

2. What is the total value of two 1-rupee coins combined?
   - (A) ₹2
   - (B) ₹3
   - (C) ₹4
   - (D) ₹5

3. What is the total value of two 5-rupee coins?
   - (A) ₹10
   - (B) ₹7
   - (C) ₹8
   - (D) ₹15

4. Whose portrait appears on Indian currency banknotes?
   - (A) Mahatma Gandhi
   - (B) Jawaharlal Nehru
   - (C) Subhas Chandra Bose
   - (D) Dr. B. R. Ambedkar

5. A pencil costs ₹5 and an eraser costs ₹3. How much total money is needed to buy both?
   - (A) ₹8
   - (B) ₹7
   - (C) ₹9
   - (D) ₹10
