### I. Multiple Choice Questions
*Reference: Textbook Pages 15 to 41 — Subtraction & Numbers 21 to 99*

1. What is the value of 8 - 4?
   - (A) 4
   - (B) 3
   - (C) 5
   - (D) 2

2. What is the result when zero is subtracted from 3? (3 - 0 = ?)
   - (A) 3
   - (B) 0
   - (C) 2
   - (D) 1

3. Which number consists of 5 Tens and 3 Ones?
   - (A) 53
   - (B) 35
   - (C) 50
   - (D) 8

4. What number is formed by the expansion 60 + 6?
   - (A) 66
   - (B) 60
   - (C) 76
   - (D) 69

5. What is the missing number in the skip counting sequence: 2, 4, 6, 8, _______?
   - (A) 10
   - (B) 9
   - (C) 12
   - (D) 11

6. What is the missing number in: 3, 6, 9, 12, _______?
   - (A) 15
   - (B) 14
   - (C) 13
   - (D) 16

7. Which number comes immediately after 49?
   - (A) 50
   - (B) 48
   - (C) 51
   - (D) 40

8. Which number comes immediately before 70?
   - (A) 69
   - (B) 71
   - (C) 68
   - (D) 60
