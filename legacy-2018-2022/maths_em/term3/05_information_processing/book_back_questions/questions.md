### I. Multiple Choice Questions
*Reference: Textbook Pages 55 to 65 — Assembling Parts & Instructions*

1. Which is an essential part of an automobile car?
   - (A) Wheel
   - (B) Wing
   - (C) Oar
   - (D) Sail

2. Which unique facial part belongs to an elephant?
   - (A) Trunk
   - (B) Fin
   - (C) Feather
   - (D) Shell

3. Connecting numbered dots from 1 to 10 in sequential order results in:
   - (A) A complete picture
   - (B) A blank page
   - (C) A subtraction table
   - (D) A coin

4. Following instructions step-by-step helps us complete a model:
   - (A) Accurately
   - (B) Incorrectly
   - (C) Randomly
   - (D) Never
