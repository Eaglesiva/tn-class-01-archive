### I. Multiple Choice Questions
*Reference: Textbook Pages 7 to 14 — Rolling and Sliding*

1. Which object rolls down smoothly when placed on an inclined ramp?
   - (A) Ball
   - (B) Mathematics textbook
   - (C) Matchbox
   - (D) Eraser

2. Which flat object slides down an inclined ramp without rolling?
   - (A) Eraser
   - (B) Lemon
   - (C) Marble
   - (D) Orange

3. Which object can both roll and slide depending on how it is placed?
   - (A) One-rupee coin
   - (B) Basketball
   - (C) Brick
   - (D) Blackboard

4. If a cylindrical water bottle is placed horizontally on a slope, what will it do?
   - (A) Roll
   - (B) Slide
   - (C) Float
   - (D) Fly
