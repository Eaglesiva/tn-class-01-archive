### II. Multiple Choice Questions
*Reference: Textbook Pages 27, 30, 33, 40, 44*

   - (B) See-saw
   - (C) Slide
   - (D) Ball

   - (A) See-saw
   - (B) Swing
   - (C) Slide
   - (D) Top

   - (A) Bat
   - (B) Racket

   - (B) A dolphin

   - (A) Red
   - (B) Blue
   - (C) White

   - (A) Yellow
   - (B) Blue

### III. Magic Words (Polite Expressions)
*Reference: Textbook Page 43 — Let us follow*

Select the appropriate polite expression for each situation:

1. When you receive a gift from someone, what should you say?
   - (A) Thank you
   - (B) Sorry
   - (C) Goodbye

2. When you accidentally hurt someone or break something, what should you say?
   - (A) Sorry
   - (B) Please
   - (C) Welcome

3. When you ask a classmate to lend a pencil, what magic word should you use?
   - (A) Please
   - (B) Sorry
   - (C) No
