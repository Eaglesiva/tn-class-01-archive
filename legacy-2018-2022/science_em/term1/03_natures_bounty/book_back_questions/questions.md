### I. Multiple Choice Questions
*Reference: Textbook Pages 29 to 42 — Nature's Bounty*

1. A big, strong plant with a thick woody trunk is called a:
   - (A) Tree
   - (B) Grass
   - (C) Herb
   - (D) Creeper

2. Which of the following flowers has a sweet fragrance?
   - (A) Jasmine
   - (B) Pumpkin flower
   - (C) Plantain flower
   - (D) Coconut flower

3. Which of the following natural foods is rich in vitamins and good for health?
   - (A) Fresh fruits
   - (B) Plastic candies
   - (C) Junk food
   - (D) Aerated soda

4. What is the National Flower of India?
   - (A) Lotus
   - (B) Rose
   - (C) Jasmine
   - (D) Hibiscus

5. Which vegetable can be eaten raw as a salad?
   - (A) Carrot
   - (B) Potato
   - (C) Yam
   - (D) Brinjal
