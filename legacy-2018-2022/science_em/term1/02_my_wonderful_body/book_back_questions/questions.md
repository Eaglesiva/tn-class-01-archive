### I. Multiple Choice Questions
*Reference: Textbook Pages 15 to 28 — Body Parts and Sense Organs*

1. Which sense organ do we use to see colourful sights and read books?
   - (A) Eyes
   - (B) Ears
   - (C) Nose
   - (D) Tongue

2. Which organ helps us taste sweet and delicious food?
   - (A) Tongue
   - (B) Nose
   - (C) Skin
   - (D) Ear

3. Which organ helps us listen to musical sounds and voices?
   - (A) Ears
   - (B) Eyes
   - (C) Hands
   - (D) Tongue

4. Which organ covers our entire body and helps us feel hot, cold, rough, and smooth?
   - (A) Skin
   - (B) Ear
   - (C) Nose
   - (D) Eye

5. Which part of our body helps us kick a football and run fast?
   - (A) Legs and feet
   - (B) Ears
   - (C) Teeth
   - (D) Nose
