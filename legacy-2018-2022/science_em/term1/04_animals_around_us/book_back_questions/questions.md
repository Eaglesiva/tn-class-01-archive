### I. Multiple Choice Questions
*Reference: Textbook Pages 43 to 64 — Animals Around Us*

1. Which domestic farm animal gives us nutritious milk?
   - (A) Cow
   - (B) Fox
   - (C) Bear
   - (D) Lion

2. Which animal is famously known as the King of the Jungle?
   - (A) Lion
   - (B) Deer
   - (C) Rabbit
   - (D) Dog

3. Which bird with beautiful colourful feathers is the National Bird of India?
   - (A) Peacock
   - (B) Crow
   - (C) Pigeon
   - (D) Myna

4. Which hardworking insect collects sweet nectar from flowers and makes honey?
   - (A) Honeybee
   - (B) Mosquito
   - (C) Housefly
   - (D) Cockroach

5. Which faithful animal guards our homes?
   - (A) Dog
   - (B) Wolf
   - (C) Leopard
   - (D) Fox
