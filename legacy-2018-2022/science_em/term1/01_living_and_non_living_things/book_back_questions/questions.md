### I. Multiple Choice Questions
*Reference: Textbook Pages 5 to 14 — Living and Non-living Things*

1. Which of the following is a living thing?
   - (A) Plant
   - (B) Stone
   - (C) Chair
   - (D) Book

2. Which of the following does a non-living thing NOT need?
   - (A) Food
   - (B) Air
   - (C) Water
   - (D) All of these

3. Which entity can move from one place to another on its own?
   - (A) Puppy
   - (B) Wooden chair
   - (C) Table
   - (D) Pebble

4. Which of the following is a man-made non-living thing?
   - (A) Bicycle
   - (B) River
   - (C) Mountain
   - (D) Sun

5. Which of the following is a natural non-living thing?
   - (A) Rock
   - (B) Car
   - (C) Pencil
   - (D) Shirt

### II. Fill in the Blanks
*Reference: Textbook Pages 6, 8, 12*

1. Living things have the ability to _______. (grow / remain unchanged)
2. A wooden table is a _______ thing. (non-living / living)
3. A cow eats grass, so it is a _______ thing. (living / non-living)
4. Living things need _______ to breathe. (air / stones)
5. Mountains, rivers, and soil are _______ non-living things. (natural / man-made)
