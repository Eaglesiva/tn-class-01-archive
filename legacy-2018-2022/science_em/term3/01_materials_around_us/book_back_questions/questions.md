### I. Multiple Choice Questions
*Reference: Textbook Pages 8 to 15 — Materials Around Us*

1. Which material is obtained from tree trunks to make tables, benches, and doors?
   - (A) Wood
   - (B) Clay
   - (C) Glass
   - (D) Plastic

2. Which material is moulded with water and baked in a kiln to make pots and lamps?
   - (A) Clay
   - (B) Steel
   - (C) Wood
   - (D) Rubber

3. Which of the following is a hard and rigid material?
   - (A) Iron nail
   - (B) Cotton ball
   - (C) Flower petal
   - (D) Foam sponge

4. Which item is soft to press and comfortable to sleep on?
   - (A) Cotton pillow
   - (B) Brick
   - (C) Stone slab
   - (D) Metal plate

5. Which fragile material breaks easily if dropped on the floor?
   - (A) Glass tumbler
   - (B) Wooden ruler
   - (C) Iron spoon
   - (D) Jute rope
