### I. Multiple Choice Questions
*Reference: Textbook Pages 16 to 24 — Our Neighbourhood*

1. The area and surroundings around our home is called our:
   - (A) Neighbourhood
   - (B) Jungle
   - (C) Riverbank
   - (D) Sky

2. Where do we go in our neighbourhood to buy groceries, fruits, and vegetables?
   - (A) Market
   - (B) Police station
   - (C) Fire station
   - (D) Hospital

3. Where do sick people go to receive medical care and treatment?
   - (A) Hospital
   - (B) Post office
   - (C) Bus terminus

4. Where do children go in the evening to swing, slide, and play games?
   - (A) Children's Park
   - (B) Hospital
   - (C) Bank
   - (D) Police station

5. Where should we deposit dry and wet waste in our neighbourhood?
   - (A) Garbage dustbin
   - (B) Roadside drain
   - (C) Street corner
   - (D) Footpath
