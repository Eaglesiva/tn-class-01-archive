### I. Multiple Choice Questions
*Reference: Textbook Pages 34 to 39 — Day and Night*

1. What shines brightly in the sky during the day, giving light and warmth to Earth?
   - (A) Sun
   - (B) Moon
   - (C) Cloud
   - (D) Star

2. What twinkling celestial bodies appear in the night sky along with the moon?
   - (A) Stars
   - (B) Suns
   - (C) Rainbows
   - (D) Kites

3. Which bird stays awake and hunts for food at night?
   - (A) Owl
   - (B) Pigeon
   - (C) Crow
   - (D) Sparrow

4. Which winged mammal flies actively during the dark night?
   - (A) Bat
   - (B) Dog
   - (C) Squirrel
   - (D) Cat

5. What dark shape is formed on the ground when an opaque object blocks light?
   - (A) Shadow
   - (B) Rain
   - (C) Smoke
   - (D) Wind
