### I. Multiple Choice Questions
*Reference: Textbook Pages 25 to 33 — Transport*

1. Which long vehicle travels on railway tracks laid on the ground?
   - (A) Train
   - (B) Bus
   - (C) Bicycle
   - (D) Car

2. Which fastest vehicle flies through the air across long distances?
   - (A) Aeroplane
   - (B) Ship
   - (C) Auto-rickshaw
   - (D) Lorry

3. Which large water vehicle sails across oceans carrying passengers and cargo?
   - (A) Ship
   - (B) Motorcycle
   - (C) Bus
   - (D) Bullock cart

4. Which marked white stripes on the road should pedestrians use to cross safely?
   - (A) Zebra crossing
   - (B) Yellow boundary
   - (C) Curved divider
   - (D) Speed breaker

5. What must a two-wheeler rider wear on their head for safety?
   - (A) Helmet
   - (B) Cap
   - (C) Sun glasses
   - (D) Ribbon
