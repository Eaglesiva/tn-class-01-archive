### I. Multiple Choice Questions
*Reference: Textbook Pages 17 to 23 — Water*

1. What is the primary natural source of freshwater on Earth?
   - (A) Rain
   - (B) Hand pump
   - (C) Water tank
   - (D) Tap

2. Which water body provides fresh drinking water to villages and towns?
   - (A) Well
   - (B) Salty ocean
   - (C) Drainage canal
   - (D) Sewage pool

3. Which of the following cannot survive without water?
   - (A) Plants, animals, and human beings
   - (B) Stones
   - (C) Books
   - (D) Wooden toys

4. What should we do to the tap while brushing our teeth?
   - (A) Turn it off
   - (B) Keep it running continuously
   - (C) Break it
   - (D) Ignore it

5. What do we use to quench our thirst and cook everyday meals?
   - (A) Clean water
   - (B) Muddy water
   - (C) Oil only
   - (D) Sand
