   - (C) Tailor
   - (D) Mason

   - (A) Doctor
   - (B) Police officer
   - (C) Postman
   - (D) Firefighter

   - (A) Farmer
   - (B) Postman

   - (A) Sanitation worker

   - (A) Pongal

### II. Fill in the Blanks
*Reference: Textbook Pages 27, 31, 35*

1. A _______ protects people and maintains law and order. (police officer / postman)
2. A _______ delivers letters and parcels to our doorstep. (postman / doctor)
3. A _______ puts out accidental fires and rescues people. (firefighter / tailor)
4. A quiet place where we go to read books is a _______. (library / market)
5. On Pongal festival, sweet _______ is prepared in a new clay pot. (sweet pongal / cake)
