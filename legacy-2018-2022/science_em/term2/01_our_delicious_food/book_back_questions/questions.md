### I. Multiple Choice Questions
*Reference: Textbook Pages 3 to 16 — Our Delicious Food*

1. What gives our body energy to play, study, and grow healthy?
   - (A) Nutritious food
   - (B) Dust
   - (C) Plastic
   - (D) Medicines only

2. Which of the following foods is obtained from plants?
   - (A) Rice and vegetables
   - (B) Milk
   - (C) Honey
   - (D) Egg

3. Which wholesome liquid food do we get from cows and buffaloes?
   - (A) Milk
   - (B) Cooking oil
   - (C) Honey
   - (D) Fruit juice

4. Which sweet and natural food is collected by honeybees?
   - (A) Honey
   - (B) Sugar
   - (C) Jaggery
   - (D) Butter

5. Which of the following is an unhealthy junk food that should be avoided?
   - (A) Deep-fried packet chips
   - (B) Boiled pulses
   - (C) Fresh guava
   - (D) Spinach soup
